What this demonstration measures

The three Earth candidates were selected from one NASADEM tile; Kilbourne is the original baseline. The lunar reference is a fixed eastward trial line in the Site04 elevation model. A low mismatch identifies a similar elevation profile; it does not establish geological equivalence, site access, or mission suitability.

The model checks 75 positions spaced about 29.76 m apart over 2,202.181 m. Relay choices are limited to those positions and links move forward along the corridor. Earth and lunar curvature use spherical radii of 6,371 km and 1,737.4 km. Counts minimize intermediate relays; ties favor greater minimum sampled interior clearance.

Links must clear the modeled ground at every sampled position. The minimum is restricted to this sampled one-dimensional problem. Smaller obstacles can be missed. The network display uses start-relative heights and exaggerates vertical scale for readability.

Nominal geometric visibility only. No radio link budget, Fresnel clearance, vegetation, buildings, field measurements, or DEM uncertainty is included in these relay counts. Candidate selection and scoring use the same Earth DEM; this is not independent validation.

Lunar reference: native projected coordinates (−4000, −6500) to (−1797.819, −6500) m. Each lunar 30 m cell averages a complete 6 × 6 block of the source 5 m elevation grid. Earth sampling uses the 30 m UTM grid.
